# The factory presets (firmware/src/presets.c): tools/gen_presets.py writes firmware/src/presets.h and
# tests/scripts/presets.txt from this file; tools/pr_level.py measures every lane and rewrites the levels (v).
#
#   = NAME                       a category (the presets after it, in this order: F001 .. F100)
#   NAME  fxN | lane | lane | lane | lane
#                                NAME 11 characters at most (A-Z 0-9 space - & ' .); fxN the effects (gen_presets.py
#                                FX: 0 dry, 1 air, 2 space, 3 echo, 4 cave), applied unless EFFECTS ON LOAD keeps the player's
#   lane: C:sound  nN aN rR pN vN [m]   or  -   (no sound)
#     C   the family: W Water, S Stones & sand, I Ice & glass, F Fire, D Wood, A Wind, T Storm, X Insects,
#         B Songbirds, Y Wild birds; the sound by its name there
#     nN  HOW MANY 0 .. 6 (0: loaded, not playing), aN HOW ACTIVE key 0 .. 8 (presets start sparse: quiet start)
#     rR  its place in the mix: F foreground, M midground, B background (pr_level.py: -6 / -12 dB under F), or a
#         number of dB under the foreground (r-8)
#     pN  PAN 0 .. 127 (64 centre); vN LEVEL 0 .. 127 (100 = as tuned; measured: pr_level.py); m muted
#
# Neighbours in a category share sounds on the same lanes where the places allow: a load from one to the next then
# swaps fewer sounds and glides the rest. Lanes, by habit: 1 the place's main source, 2 its detail, 3 the air, 4 a
# voice or an accent.
