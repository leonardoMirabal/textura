A mid/side section, the last effect, after the reverb.

- M/S: a new page after SHIMMER, the last of the effects (FX: NEXT > M/S on SHIMMER, then round to COMPRESSOR).
  K1 MID and K2 SIDE (−INF, −57 to +6 dB), K3 WIDTH (0 to 200 %), K4 MONO. At 0 dB with MONO off it leaves the
  sound exactly as it was; every change glides. The bottom line shows L, R, M and S meters and the correlation.
- The limiter comes after it, so a boost never clips.
- Saved with presets: older presets load it at 0 dB, 100 %, MONO off. EFFECTS ON LOAD covers it as the other effects.
- CC 25 / 26 / 27 / 28 on channel 5 (MID, SIDE, WIDTH, MONO); NRPN scope 4, 96 to 99; MID and SIDE are matrix
  targets.
