Headphones, a dice that knows the stereo image, and many fixes from a full scan of the FM-1 and the plugin.

- OUTPUT: SPEAKERS or HEADPHONES. HEADPHONES places each lane by the delay between the ears and the head's shadow
  (binaural), with AMOUNT and HEAD SIZE in SETTINGS. On the FM-1 it runs on both cores without late blocks.
- RANDOM, the dice: per lane and for everything, on the FM-1 (OCT- + OCT+) and in the plugin. It keeps the stereo
  image (pans and M/S stay balanced) and rolls level cards near their defaults. A general roll fills the empty lanes.
- Reverb: HALL, INFINITE, DARK, DRIFT, SWELL and PLATE now keep their lows in the middle like a real room, the mids
  and highs as wide as before. They no longer vanish when SIDE is turned down; DARK's highs no longer cancel in mono.
- SHIMMER is audible, with an even knob.
- M/S: WIDTH is its own control.
- Lanes: HOW ACTIVE works over its whole range, the first note comes soon, lanes keep their activity over an hour.
- Events start on their own sample (no 5.8 ms grid).
- Fixes: the limiter's peaks between samples, ICICLES and CRICKETS levels, the storm builds before its first flash,
  hail on a car without the big opening stone, GRAIN TONE / SPREAD, DARK MOD, the FM-1's screen text.
- EFFECTS ON LOAD: NEVER by default on the FM-1 (your effects stay), ALWAYS in the plugin.
- SAVE picks a free slot and a name; presets have richer effects and a DREAMS category.
- Plugin: the selected lane and the effects in two titled zones, a SOUND dice for the lane's cards, MAP links a card
  with one click, an M/S view, a first-run tour and HELP.
