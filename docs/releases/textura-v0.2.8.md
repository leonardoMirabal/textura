Textura is public, with its emulator in the browser: play it at https://leonardomirabal.github.io/textura/ and install from the same page.

- The emulator: a keyboard map under the keys (lanes 1-4 on the black-key groups, HOW MANY and HOW ACTIVE on the white keys), four steps to start and a short quick guide; the full control texts are in docs/MANUAL.md.
- Install on your FM-1 from the emulator page (the browser installer), as from the hub or the FM-1 Loader.
- README: the device and its keyboard guide first, where to find Textura, credits and support.
- The firmware's sound and controls are unchanged from 0.2.7.
