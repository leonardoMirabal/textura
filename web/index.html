Tap "Start sound" (browsers need a tap before they play audio).
QUICK GUIDE
BLACK KEYS
by their groups (3, 2, 3, 2): lanes 1–4, shown on LANDSCAPE (F#5: nothing; a lane's sound: SEQ, the sound browser); held 0.6 s: that lane muted, or unmuted (it plays on, so it comes back at once; MUTED on its card; a scene keeps it)
F3–E4
how many: off, 1, 2, 4, 10, 19, 32
F4–G5
how active: rare .. busy (each key sounds the same more)
SEQ
SOUNDS: the sound browser for the selected lane: the families, then their sounds; ALGORITHM moves (the sound plays after 0.4 s while the lane plays), SELECT jumps family, EDIT opens / picks, HOME back / cancel. Any sound on any lane, the same one on two lanes too
ALGORITHM
landscape (SOUND: opens the sound browser; DRIVE, REVERB: the type; FILTER: the slope; COMPRESSOR: on / off; GRAIN: freeze; LFO: which LFO; MATRIX: which slot)
KNOB 1–4
the volumes of lanes 1–4 (MIXER: lanes 1–4; other pages: the cards)
SELECT
pages, first to last (no wrap)
HOME
LANDSCAPE; again: back to the page it came from (if that page was not left another way)
EDIT
the selected lane's sound (again: its next page)
FX
drive / filter / compressor / grain delay / reverb (the mix's chain, in its order: one of each on all the lanes together; the reverb takes what the grain delay gives). FILTER: a band, LOW CUT (a high-pass) and HIGH CUT (a low-pass), OFF at their ends; LOW CUT above HIGH CUT: a narrow band between the two; RESONANCE peaks both edges; SLOPE 12 / 24 dB (also ALGORITHM). MIX: DRY..WET (DRY the sound alone, WET the effect alone, between at equal power)
GLO
tempo / MIDI (clock in: AUTO follows a clock while one comes). TEMPO 2/2: SCENE TEMPO (a scene brings its tempo) and SCENE FX (KEEP, the default: a scene keeps the drive, filter, compressor, delay and reverb as they are, their tails ringing on; RECALL: it brings its own)
LFO
the 8 LFOs' pages; held while a card turns: that LFO onto the card (the turn its depth, back to 0 removes it; here: a click latches learn for 3 s, turn a knob; a second click: the LFO pages)
ARP
the mod matrix: 16 slots, source > target, depth, spread (a modulated card shows ~ and its value now)
OCT− OCT+
previous / next lane: SOUND stays on that page, other pages go to LANDSCAPE (PLAY keys: octave)
SEL
keys: landscape / play
REC
all lanes stop / start
PLAY
tap tempo; held: start / stop
PRESETS
the presets browser: ten categories of factory presets (F001–F100) and USER (your U01–U16), then their presets; the one under the cursor plays as you stop turning, EDIT keeps it, HOME cancels
SAVE
save the scene: PRESETS or K1 picks U01–U16, SAVE again saves, HOME cancels; held: undo the last load (again: redo). Kept in this browser; MIDI program change picks one too (0–9, 16–31)

Four lanes play at once; the keys' lights blink with each lane's activity.

Computer keys: A–; the keys F3–A4 · Z X lane / octave · Space all on / off · ← → pages · ↑ ↓ landscapes · knobs: drag or wheel · MIDI notes (channel 1) play the selected lane; CC and NRPN: lanes 1–4 on channels 1–4, the effects on 5
