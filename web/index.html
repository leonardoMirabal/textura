Tap "Start sound" (browsers need a tap before they play audio).
1 · PICK A LANE

Tap a black key. Each group is one lane: 3 keys for lane 1, 2 for lane 2, 3 for lane 3, 2 for lane 4. Hold one for 0.6 s to mute that lane, again to unmute it.

2 · HOW MANY

The seven left white keys set how many sources play: off, 1, 2, 4, 10, 19 or 32.

3 · HOW ACTIVE

The nine right white keys set how often they sound, from rare to busy. Four lanes play at once.

PLAY MODE

SEL turns the keys into a pitched keyboard for the selected lane; OCT− and OCT+ then shift the octave.

Computer keys: A–; the keys F3–A4 · Z X lane / octave · Space all on / off · ← → pages · ↑ ↓ landscapes · knobs: drag or wheel · MIDI notes: channel 1 the selected lane, channels 1–4 lanes 1–4 (GLO: MIDI IN); CC and NRPN: lanes 1–4 on their channels, the effects on 5
